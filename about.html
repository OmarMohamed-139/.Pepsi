Health & Nutritional Information

We believe in transparency and providing options that fit your lifestyle. Whether you enjoy the classic bold taste or prefer our zero-sugar alternatives, we ensure quality in every sip.

  • Ingredients: Carbonated water, sugar/high fructose corn syrup, caramel color, phosphoric acid, caffeine, and natural flavors.
  • Pepsi Zero Sugar: Enjoy the exact same iconic bold taste with zero sugar and zero calories for a lighter choice.
  • Balanced Lifestyle: We support mindful consumption and encourage enjoying Pepsi as part of a balanced diet and active life.
